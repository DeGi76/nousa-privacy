Beta privacy policy

Your information in Nousa

Updated October 12, 2026 · Demetrios Gianniris, developer of Nousa

This policy describes the current beta. Included cloud AI through a Nousa relay is planned and is not part of this build. This policy will be updated before that service becomes available.

Notes, tasks and documents

Nousa stores your work locally. With iCloud enabled, supported content and settings sync through Apple under your Apple ID. Local exports and backups may also be written to locations you choose. People you invite to a shared workspace can access its shared content. Files exported to shared folders follow that folder’s access rules.

Health data

Nousa Health is optional and off until you turn it on. It can bring in data you choose from Apple Health (HealthKit) on your iPhone, from WHOOP when you connect your WHOOP account, and from your medical records when you connect a health system’s patient portal (such as MyChart) or import records files. It also keeps the food diary, recipes and goals you enter.

Health data is stored on your devices and in your own iCloud account under your Apple ID, like the rest of your Nousa data, so it appears on your other devices signed in to the same account. The developer has no server that receives it and cannot read it.

Health data is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with data brokers or other third parties. Data from Apple Health is used only to show you your health information in Nousa, as Apple’s HealthKit rules require. Nousa does not write to Apple Health unless you turn that on.

WHOOP. When you connect WHOOP, Nousa asks WHOOP for read-only access to your recovery, cycles (strain), sleep, workouts and body measurements, and keeps a copy only if you allow it. The WHOOP sign-in is kept in the Keychain on your device. WHOOP data is not sent to any AI provider. You can disconnect at any time, which revokes Nousa’s access with WHOOP, and you can choose to remove the WHOOP data Nousa kept.

Medical records. Records come directly from the health system you sign in to, using its patient-authorized access (SMART on FHIR). The sign-in is kept in the Keychain on your device.

Nous and health data. Health information is sent to the AI provider you chose only when you allow it in Health › Settings, and only to answer your request. Nousa provides information to help you understand your own data; it is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Your controls. In Health › Settings you can export all of your health data, delete it, or turn Health off. In iOS Settings › Health you can change or remove what Nousa may read from Apple Health.

Optional AI

With a cloud provider connected, Nous sends prompts and relevant note, task, calendar, document or tool-result context directly to that provider using your API key. The current cloud options include Anthropic, OpenAI and Cerebras. The provider’s terms, retention practices and account settings apply.

Enabled automatic features can also make AI requests, including Home summaries, document extraction and email filing. Cloud requests are not limited to pressing Send. Review “What Nous does on its own” and the provider selection in Settings › Nous.

Apple’s on-device model is available only on supported systems. Ollama requests use the server address you configure; a remote address sends requests to that server. Local capabilities differ from cloud capabilities. Keys and connection tokens are stored in Keychain, and supported credentials may sync through iCloud Keychain.

Connections and lookups

Optional Microsoft, Notion, Todoist and other integrations use the accounts and permissions you connect. Read and write operations transfer content to the relevant service. Product and barcode lookups, saved web sources and web clipping contact external services to retrieve information.

Scanning and voice

Document OCR uses Apple’s Vision framework on the device. AI-assisted extraction may send recognized text to the selected model. Voice features use system speech services; on-device availability depends on system support. Spoken requests may then be processed by Nous like typed requests.

Diagnostics and support

The app does not include an advertising or marketing analytics service. Local diagnostic logs may contain operation details or generated text. Review any diagnostics before sending them to support. If you email the developer, the message and any attachments are processed to respond to your request. Apple may separately process TestFlight, crash and purchase information under its own policies.

Your controls

You can change AI providers, disable automatic AI features, disconnect services and manage system permissions. Use the app’s export, backup and deletion tools to manage your content. Removing a connection does not necessarily delete copies already stored by that service. Separately manage exported files, backups and shared copies. Provider retention is governed by the relevant provider.

Children and changes

Nousa is not directed at children under 13. Changes to how data is handled will be reflected in this policy before the affected features are offered.

Contact

For privacy questions or support correspondence, email support@nousa.global. Please avoid including private note content unless it is needed to explain your request.
